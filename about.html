About Me

I'm a BBA student at IIT Patna, preparing for a career in management consulting — with a sharp eye on strategy, market dynamics, and international affairs. I enjoy solving complex problems and simplifying them into clear, actionable frameworks that drive decisions.

I started in the world of finance through trading, which taught me discipline, risk management, and the importance of timing. While I continue trading actively, I realized I wanted to work on bigger, more collaborative challenges — the kind that require strategic thinking, communication, and real-world impact. That led me to consulting.

Beyond business and markets, I have a growing interest in geopolitical analysis and peacebuilding — topics I explore through writing, research, and internships. I'm especially interested in how economics, politics, and narrative shape global conflict and cooperation.

Outside of work, I enjoy long walks, films, working out, and spending time with friends and family. I believe that good work comes from a balanced life — one where you're present, curious, and not always sprinting. No point making truckloads of money if you can't even take a break for a great cup of coffee :)
